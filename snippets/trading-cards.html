TradingCards

System Design
Graphic Design
Production

One system. Every player.

Role
Art direction, graphic design, system design, production
Year
2022–26
Project
A modular sports card system built to flex across teams, colourways, player data and collectible formats. Every series shares one construction, a photo window, number, name plate, team mark and stat panel, then takes on its own finish, from gold all-star to chrome. Printed on thick trading-card stock and square-cut. Made on staff at Stafford Studios for youth hockey associations.
Deliverables
Card series and parallels, player photography, designed card backs, print production

Players shown are fictional. Team marks are used for concept purposes only.
